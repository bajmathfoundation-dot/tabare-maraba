Terms & Conditions

1. Membership Obligations: All members must abide by the cooperative's constitution and operational guidelines.

2. Dues & Contributions: Members are required to pay registration fees and monthly dues as determined by the cooperative's management.

3. Conduct: Members must maintain professional conduct and uphold the reputation of TABARE Cooperative at all times.

4. Safety: Drivers must comply with all road safety regulations and ensure their tricycles are roadworthy.

5. Termination: The cooperative reserves the right to terminate membership for violation of rules or misconduct.

6. Data Privacy: Personal information will be kept confidential and used only for cooperative purposes.

7. Dispute Resolution: Any disputes shall be resolved internally through the cooperative's dispute resolution mechanism.
