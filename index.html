About Cooperative

TABARE is a community-based transport cooperative operating from Maraba town Dass Local Government Area in Bauchi State. We bring together local tricycle operators under a unified, professional system.

  • Serving Maraba, Bauchi metropolis & surrounding areas
  • Promoting local economic empowerment
  • Professional and reliable transport services
